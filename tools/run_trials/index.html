    Common gotchas

    Verbatim from phases/phase-2-experiments.md. Click to expand.
    • Battery dropping mid-session — record battery % at start; ROSbot motor performance degrades below ~30%.
    • Logger session_id not applied — if the launch param didn't take, all CSVs collide. Verify the filename matches your session_id before each run.
    • TwistStamped timestamps drift — keep robot and laptop on the same NTP source; otherwise latency-from-decode-to-cmd_vel readings will be noisy.
    • Cards reflective under overhead lights — print on matte paper or laminate matte side out. Glare destroys CLAHE.
    • Don't change cmd_cooldown_s between trials — keep all baseline parameters identical across T1–T5 so V.2.1 numbers are comparable.
